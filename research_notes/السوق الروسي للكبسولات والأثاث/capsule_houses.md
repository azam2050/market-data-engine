# Russian "space capsule" modular houses (капсульные дома / дом-капсула / Apple Cabin): manufacturers, importers, prices, market

Research date: 2026-10-20. Contacts were taken from each company's own site (raw HTML pulled with curl and parsed), unless the note says otherwise. Per-m² prices are my own arithmetic: list price ÷ stated area. Prices are "from" (от) list prices and usually exclude delivery, foundation and utility hook-ups unless the note says they are included.

Legend: **[MFR-RU]** = says it manufactures in Russia. **[IMP/CN-factory]** = says it owns or represents a Chinese factory and imports. **[RESELL/agent]** = dealer, buying agent or catalogue reseller. **[ADJ]** = an adjacent product, such as a panoramic glamping module that is not an aluminium capsule.

## Q1. Which Russian companies manufacture capsule houses locally? (company, city, site, phones, email, Telegram/WhatsApp, INN, models, RUB price, RUB/m²)

### Takeaway
Few firms make true aluminium-shell capsules in Russia. Most "capsule" sellers either import from China or rebadge Chinese units. Firms that claim Russian production include LUNA / SpaceDom (Leningrad oblast), Apple House (IP Skiba, Russia), Shattl (Perm), Qwik Dom (factory in Simferopol), and the generalists DP-Module (Ekaterinburg) and Avista Modul. Russian-made steel-frame capsules list at about 125–136k RUB/m² (≈2.7–4.7 M RUB for 20–38 m²). That is close to the price of imported Chinese units sold turnkey in Russia.

### Inferences
- "Собственное производство" claims are hard to verify from websites. Shattl, LUNA, Apple House and Qwik Dom are the most credible local capsule makers found. Their prices (≈125–136k ₽/m²) sit well above Chinese factory-direct offers (≈67–72k ₽/m², see Q2). The local premium probably reflects small-batch steel and aluminium fabrication, not cheaper inputs.
- DP-Module, Avista and Modultur make capsule-like modules. They do not make aluminium "space capsules".

### Gaps
- INNs for LUNA, Shattl, Qwik Dom and Avista were not found on their sites. rusprofile.ru was not queried within the tool budget.
- These contacts could not be verified: Qwik Dom (VK captcha) and CUPSUL HOUSE Krasnodar (vk.com/capsulehouse_south; the VK snippet only says it builds capsule houses in the Krasnodar area, Southern federal district and Crimea).

## Q2. Which companies import Chinese capsules into Russia, at what delivered RUB prices, and from which Chinese brands?

### Takeaway
Importers are the larger group. They are concentrated in Vladivostok/Primorye, Moscow, Krasnodar, Irkutsk, Novosibirsk and St Petersburg. Delivered turnkey prices in Russia run about 1.9–5.6 M RUB for 18–38 m² units, which is roughly 130–180k ₽/m². Factory-direct offers from Vladivostok run about 67–72k ₽/m². Few sellers name the Chinese factory. Those named are Lijian Construction Engineering (Lemnispace), the Shanfeng factory (Farpost listing), a Shenyang factory (Well House) and "Nova" (Дом везде).

## Q3. Typical RUB/m², lead times, materials, demand drivers, regions

### Takeaway
Turnkey capsules in Russia typically cost **≈125–180k ₽/m²**: about 2–6 M ₽ for an 18–38 m² unit. Budget factory-direct Chinese units start around 67k ₽/m², and small premium pods can reach 200k+ ₽/m². Lead time is typically 45–70 days. The standard build is a galvanised or stainless steel frame, aluminium cladding, PU foam or mineral-wool insulation, and tempered double-glazed panoramic glass. Glamping is the main demand driver.

### Cited Findings
- An average 28 m² capsule cost 2 M ₽ in 2024 (≈71k ₽/m²). About 70% of modular houses in Russia are imported from China. Logistics add up to 20% of cost and customs about 15% — [hotel.report](https://ru.hotel.report/technology/biznes-na-emociyah.-prizhivutsya-li-v-rossii-kapsulnye-doma)
- The same article quotes Aleksei Kharitonenko of ООО «ДКС Империя» calling for subsidies and simpler certification. It also cites experts who call capsules a niche product — [hotel.report](https://ru.hotel.report/technology/biznes-na-emociyah.-prizhivutsya-li-v-rossii-kapsulnye-doma)
- Another source puts a basic capsule from 1.8 M ₽, production at 1–2 months and assembly at up to 10 days — [domsvai.ru](https://domsvai.ru/articles/kapsulnye-doma-plyusy-i-minusy-tehnologii/)

## Q4. Glamping and capsule market size and trends, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Glamping demand in Russia roughly doubled in 2025. Moscow oblast, Tatarstan and Leningrad oblast lead bookings. Sources disagree sharply on how many glamping sites exist.

### Cited Findings
- **Bookings:**
  - Yandex Travel: glamping bookings for 1 May–30 Sep 2025 stays doubled year on year; winter 2025/26 bookings were up 101%; the peak average night was 16,640 ₽ (+5%) — [Yandex Travel](https://travel.yandex.ru/pro/glemping-turizm-v-rossii/)
  - Rossiyskaya Gazeta: May–Sep 2025 bookings up 72% on 2024, Oct–Dec up 52%; Level.Travel trips up about 13% — [RG, 8 Dec 2025](https://rg.ru/2025/12/08/v-rossii-vdvoe-vyros-spros-na-glemping.html)
- **Regions:**
  - Share of bookings and average bill: Moscow oblast 12% (avg 13.4k ₽), Tatarstan 11% (12.4k ₽), Leningrad oblast 8% (10.1k ₽) — [RG](https://rg.ru/2025/12/08/v-rossii-vdvoe-vyros-spros-na-glemping.html)
  - Moscow oblast had 14% of bookings in another report — [RBC Realty](https://realty.rbc.ru/news/687942fd9a79472b47008b49)
  - Every fourth glamping site in summer 2025 was in Moscow oblast — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.ru/biznes/542101-popularnost-otdyha-v-glempingah-sredi-rossian-vyrosla-vdvoe)
- **Number of sites:** the number of glamping sites rose 67% in 2025 versus 2024 — [TASS, search snippet; page blocked](https://tass.ru/ekonomika/26925449)
  - Bnovo: about 550 operating glamping sites (66% year-round), forecast to reach 700 by end-2024 — [Bnovo](https://bnovo.ru/blog/glamping-trend-that-conquers-russia/)
  - **Conflict:** an older Inc. Russia forecast expected only 250 sites, 460k tourists and 525 M ₽ turnover for 2025 — [Inc. Russia](https://incrussia.ru/understand/glamping/)
- **State funding:** the government funds glamping construction, including in Adygea and Stavropol — [Expert South](https://expertsouth.ru/news/kakoe-mesto-zaymut-glempingi-na-turisticheskom-rynke-/)

### Inferences
- Glamping growth of roughly 1.5–2× a year in bookings, plus per-room subsidies, is the main demand pull for capsule units. The fact that new importers keep appearing (Voyager, SpaceVoyager, Lemnispace, Shanfeng) suggests supply is following that demand.

### Gaps
- No data was found on the number of capsule units sold in Russia, or on the size of the capsule-house segment in RUB.
- The TASS article could not be fetched (HTTP 403), so the 67% figure comes from its search snippet only.
