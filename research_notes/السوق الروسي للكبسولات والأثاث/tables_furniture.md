### Inferences
- Budget ЛДСП TV stands and coffee tables are concentrated in the Пенза–Кузнецк cluster (Стендмебель, Мир Мебели, ЛЕКО, РВК) and around Нижний Новгород (Олмеко). These factories sell through regional wholesale dealers plus marketplace accounts. They are the most practical sources for low-cost "plasma tables" (TV stands).
- Loft metal-wood and epoxy pieces come from small workshops in Moscow and СПб, sold at roughly 27k–86k RUB retail for TV stands, or priced per quote. This is a premium, made-to-order niche, not volume supply.
- Woodville shows that part of the "Russian" occasional-table assortment is actually imported from China or Malaysia and re-sold wholesale.

### Gaps

## 2. Price bands on Wildberries / Ozon / Яндекс Маркет

### Takeaway
Only rough data was found. Wildberries TV-stand listings cluster around ~3,000–8,000 RUB after discount, with nominal "old" prices often 3–4x higher (discounts of 50–70%). Loft metal-wood TV stands start at ~27,000 RUB at independent makers. Woodville-type coffee tables retail from ~10,000 RUB. I found no systematic Ozon or Яндекс Маркет price bands.

### Cited Findings
- Wildberries TV-stand listings show heavy discounts. Example: 5,342 RUB from an old price of 22,008 RUB. Typical listed prices are ~3,000–8,000 RUB (search snapshot, not sales statistics) — [Wildberries tag page](https://www.wildberries.ru/catalog/dom/mebel/mebel-dlya-gostinoy/tumby-pod-tv/tags/shkaf-pod-tv); [WB «тумба на колёсах»](https://www.wildberries.ru/catalog/tags/tumba-pod-televizor-na-kolesah)
- Loft metal-wood TV stands cost 27,000–86,000 RUB at Loft-fabrika (СПб) — [loft-fabrika.ru](https://loft-fabrika.ru/catalog/tumbyi/)
- Coffee table «Роб D-450» (Woodville) retails from 10,458 RUB — [mebelstol.ru](https://mebelstol.ru/category/tables/coffee_tables/sort-rubric-woodville/)
- Шатура shows a 27,060 RUB unit price, discounted from 45,100 — [shatura.com](https://www.shatura.com/stores/voronezh/)
- Chinese TV stands are sold to Russia with 30–90 day delivery (cross-border channel) — [e-mall.su](https://e-mall.su/category/mebel/mebel-dlya-gostinoy/tv-tumba/)

### Inferences
- Tentative bands for TV stands:
  - Budget: under ~8k RUB (ЛДСП, marketplace).
  - Mid: ~8–30k RUB (branded factory and chain collections).
  - Premium: ~27k–90k+ RUB (loft metal/solid wood, made to order).
- Coffee tables probably follow a similar shape, from ~2–5k RUB budget to 10k+ for marble/metal designs, but this is not verified.

### Gaps
- No MPStats or Moneyplace data on sales volume or price distribution for these subcategories.
- Ozon and Яндекс Маркет prices were not sampled.
- Epoxy coffee-table prices in Russia: none found. Workshops quote on request.

## 3. Market structure: size, imports, clusters, materials, marketplaces, trends

### Takeaway
Rosstat-based figures put 2025 furniture output at about 547.6 bn RUB (+4.9%), or 98 m units (+1.1%). Household furniture fell 3.3%, and office furniture is what holds the market up. The industry association expects downward revisions. Imports fell from ~41% of the market (2020) to ~19–25% (2025), with China dominant (~67% of imports). About 80% of furniture purchases in 2025 were made online.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 output was 98 m units (+1.1% y/y): household 71.9 m (−3.3%), office 26.1 m (+15.6%). Value was 547.6 bn RUB (+4.9%). The АМДПР president says that under Rosstat's pre-summer-2025 methodology, volume fell nearly 10%, and expects revisions down — [Фонтанка, 11 Mar 2026](https://www.fontanka.ru/2026/03/11/76305549/)
- Alto Consulting estimates 2025 production at 531.4 bn RUB (−6.6% y/y, +33.6% vs 2022). This conflicts with the Rosstat-based +4.9% — [Alto Consulting](https://alto-group.ru/new/21023-obzor-rynka-mebeli-v-rossii-2025-obem-snizilsja-na-66-no-vyros-na-336-k-2022-godu.html) (page returned HTTP 500; figure from search snippet)
- BusinesStat puts 2025 sales at 111 m units, 3 m fewer than 2024, and says domestic production holds over 70% of the market — [BusinesStat](https://businesstat.ru/russia/durable_goods/furniture_interior/) (snippet)
- Residential categories are declining (kitchens and mattresses worst) because of high consumer-loan rates and fewer new-flat purchases. Первая мебельная фабрика's B2C share fell from 62% to ~52% — [Фонтанка](https://www.fontanka.ru/2026/03/11/76305549/)
- Import share fell from 41.3% (2020) to 18.7% (end-2025), per Деловой профиль. АМДПР estimates 20–25%, about 130 bn RUB, of which ~30–40 bn comes from "unfriendly" countries — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu); [Деловой профиль](https://delprof.ru/press-center/open-analytics/analiz-strategicheskikh-riskov-mebelnogo-sektora-v-2025-godu/)
- China supplies over 67% of imports (2024–2025), mainly in the low and mass segments. Belarus and Turkey compete in the mid segment. The low-priced segment is 55.9% of imports, supplied mainly by China, Belarus and Poland — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu); search snippets from [Деловой профиль](https://delprof.ru/press-center/open-analytics/analiz-strategicheskikh-riskov-mebelnogo-sektora-v-2025-godu/)
- Chinese makers hold ~30–35% of budget case furniture by volume — [sponsr.ru study](https://sponsr.ru/dbka/118928/Issledovanie_rynka_mebeli_v_Rossii_sadovaya_korpusnaya_i_kuhonnaya_mebel_20182029/) (snippet; paid study)
- The RBC research shop claims imports were 2.8x domestic production in 2024. This conflicts with every other source, so treat it as unreliable — [РБК Исследования](https://marketing.rbc.ru/research/45278/)
- Customs raised the duty on Chinese furniture fittings from 0% to 55.65% in autumn 2024. Tariffs of 35–50% on furniture from non-friendly countries are under discussion (currently 9–11%) — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu)
- ЛДСП (particleboard) costs rose 96% over 2020–2024 — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu)
- About 80% of furniture was bought online in 2025 (T-Data). Furniture sales on marketplaces split as: case furniture 45.5%, kitchens 20%, upholstered 15.2% (RBC) — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu)
- Q1 2026 sales fell ~10% y/y (АМДПР). Prices rose up to 15% in some categories, and a further 10–15% rise is possible in mass segments. Premium is holding up — [Shopandmall](https://shopandmall.ru/articles/novaa-mebelnaa-karta-rossii-kak-menaetsa-rynok-v-2026-godu)
- Northwest Federal District output grew 19.8% in value to 59.2 bn RUB, driven by B2B — [Фонтанка](https://www.fontanka.ru/2026/03/11/76305549/)
- Production clusters seen in the sources: Пенза/Кузнецк (many case-furniture factories listed by aggregators), Нижегородская обл. (Олмеко), Калининград (Лазурит, Diamir), Подмосковье (Шатура, Woodville warehouses) and СПб (loft workshops) — [meb100.ru](https://www.meb100.ru/penza/mebelnye-fabriki-opt); company pages above

### Inferences
- TV stands and coffee tables sit in "case furniture" (корпусная), which is the largest marketplace furniture category at 45.5%. Price competition is therefore driven by marketplaces, and budget Chinese supply competes there directly.
- Material mix for the volume segment is ЛДСП bodies with МДФ fronts (as at Мир Мебели). Metal-plus-solid-wood is the loft/premium niche.

### Gaps
- No separate market size for occasional tables or TV stands.
- No Belarus or Turkey import shares by value.
- Ульяновск and Воронеж clusters: no cluster-level data was found in this session.
- No quantified marketplace share for Wildberries vs Ozon vs Я.Маркет in furniture.

## 4. Export from Russia (incl. Gulf/Saudi) and import of Russian furniture

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of meaningful Russian furniture exports to the Gulf. Russian furniture exports reportedly fell ~40% after IKEA left and EU markets closed, and producers turned to the CIS (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Azerbaijan). Overall Russia–UAE and Russia–Saudi trade is growing, but furniture is not among the listed export goods.

### Cited Findings
- АМДПР reported a ~40% fall in Russian furniture exports, linked to IKEA's exit and sanctions. The article is older (not 2025) — [new-retail.ru](https://new-retail.ru/novosti/retail/obem_eksporta_mebeli_iz_rossii_upal_pochti_na_406815/)
- Producers redirected exports to Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Azerbaijan — [industrymebel.ru](https://industrymebel.ru/business/mebel-na-novyh-beregah-izuchaem-dostupnye-eksportnye-napravleniya/) (snippet)
- Russia–UAE trade exceeded $12 bn in 2025. Furniture is not among the main export items (gold, minerals, oil products, metals, grain, oils, fish) — [TAdviser](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D1%8F:%D0%A2%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B3%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BB%D1%8F_%D0%A0%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%B8_%D0%B8_%D0%9E%D0%90%D0%AD) (snippet)
- Russia–Saudi trade exceeded $4 bn in 2025, a record (Минпромторг). There is no furniture breakdown — [unotrans.com](https://unotrans.com/cases/stati/eksport-iz-rossii-v-saudovskuyu-araviyu.html) (snippet)
- Country-by-country export tables for wooden and kitchen furniture (2021–2025) exist in paid Alto Consulting reports — [Alto Consulting](https://alto-group.ru/otchot/rossija/4162-import-i-jeksport-derevjannoj-mebeli-v-rossii-v-2021-g.html)

### Inferences
- For a Gulf buyer, Russian budget ЛДСП TV stands are unlikely to beat Chinese or Turkish FOB pricing. The plausible niches are solid-wood, loft metal-wood and epoxy made-to-order pieces, and Russia-specific designs. Shipping, sanctions-related payment friction and certification (SASO/SABER in Saudi Arabia) would apply; these were not researched here.

### Gaps
- No ФТС figures for HS 9403 exports to the UAE or Saudi Arabia in 2025.
- No Russian factory with a confirmed Gulf distributor was found.
- Payment and logistics routes were not researched.
