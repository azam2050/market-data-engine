# Russian manufacturers of cabins and huts: A-frames, barnhouse modules, glamping domes, arched cabins, modular mini-houses and house kits (2025-2026)

Method note: contacts were taken straight from each company's live homepage HTML on 2026-10-20 (curl fetch, phones/emails/Telegram/WhatsApp pulled from `tel:`/`mailto:`/link markup). Prices come from the catalogue text on those pages or from search-result snippets of the same site. "Unverified" means the item was seen only in a third-party snippet or rating, or the page did not state it clearly. Prices are usually "от" (starting) prices and often leave out delivery and foundation. The per-m² figures I calculated are in the Inferences sections. Capsule houses with aluminium shells are excluded. Some capsule makers (SpaceVoyager, Volferda, Well-House, Apple House) turned up in the fetch but belong to the other researcher.

## Q1. Company directory (name, city, site, contacts, products, prices, lead time, delivery)

### Takeaway
I verified 30 producers from their own websites. The market is very fragmented. A-frames and arched/dome kits are mostly made by small sawmill-based firms in the Novgorod region (Pestovo), Leningrad region and Krasnodar. Ready-made modules (barnhouse and glamping) are dominated by DublDom, DP-Module, Woodnext, Pokanetdoma, DoorHan and Modyl. Typical turnkey prices for small glamping units (16-45 m²) are 0.7-4.7 M RUB.

### Inferences
- About 30 of these firms have their contacts confirmed from their own sites. Rows 10, 13, 18, 32 and 37 need a phone check.
- Regional clusters:
  - Novgorod region (Pestovo): many low-cost timber/A-frame builders, all selling to Moscow and SPb.
  - Leningrad region / SPb: A-frame kits and barnhouse modules.
  - Krasnodar: Zelenyi Mir kits, modular banyas, Metalwell.
  - Urals (Yekaterinburg): DublDom-Ural, DP-Module, Modyl.

## Q4. Market trends 2024-2026: glamping boom, state support, sanctions, demand

### Takeaway
Glamping grew fast in 2022-2025: bookings doubled at some platforms, the average check rose about 18-20%, and winter demand rose 52%. The state funds modular non-capital accommodation through the national tourism project (subsidy up to 1.5 M ₽ per room; 4 bn ₽ in 2025, 5.5 bn ₽ each in 2026-2027). By 2026 there are signs of cooling in some regions: in Sverdlovsk region occupancy fell 17% while prices rose, attributed to a higher tax burden.

### Cited Findings
- The Glamping Association's 2025 results:
  - Glamping is still under 1% of all out-of-town accommodation in Russia after rapid growth in 2022-24.
  - The average check rose 20% year on year, ranging from a peak of 29,937 ₽ in January to 21,605 ₽ in September.
  - One platform reports bookings up 13% and an average check of 7,900 ₽.
  - Another reports warm-season bookings up 72%.
  - Bronevik reports bookings roughly doubled in 2025 and the average check up 18.4% (19,271 → 22,816 ₽).
  - Source: [glamping-association.ru](https://glamping-association.ru/itogirinka_glempingov2025)
- Bronevik-based summary for 2025:
  - Summer demand doubled versus 2024; autumn-winter rose 52%.
  - Moscow region has 48-56 glamping sites and 14% of bookings (Avito Travel).
  - Leningrad region has 54+ glamping sites.
  - Voronezh demand grew 3.6×; Nizhny Novgorod rose 204%.
  - Source: [rosinvesthotel.ru](https://www.rosinvesthotel.ru/hotel-blog/statistika-zagruzki-glempingov-po-regionam-rossii-za-2025-god/)
- Cooling in 2026 (Sverdlovsk region, January-May 2026):
  - The average price at glamping sites and country bases was about 11.3k ₽ per room-night, up 11.9%.
  - Occupancy was 35.8%, down 17%.
  - RevPAR fell 8.1% to 4k ₽.
  - The industry cites a higher tax burden and expects a wave of glamping sites put up for sale.
  - In 2024, investors received 400 M ₽ for building glamping sites in the region.
  - Source: [RBC Yekaterinburg, 4 June 2026](https://ekb.rbc.ru/ekb/freenews/6a2131c69a794771bd952878)
- State support:
  - The federal programme subsidises the purchase and installation of modular non-capital accommodation (МНСР) — [government.ru](http://government.ru/docs/54746/)
  - The subsidy is capped at 1.5 M ₽ per room — [tourismkchr.ru](https://tourismkchr.ru/news/1515); [docs.cntd.ru](https://docs.cntd.ru/document/727709328/titles/A960NO)
  - Budget: 4 bn ₽ in 2025 and 5.5 bn ₽ in each of 2026 and 2027; nothing planned yet for 2028. The national tourism project gets 61.4 bn ₽ in 2026 — [RSPP/TASS, 29 Sep 2025](https://rspp.ru/events/news/na-natsproekt-po-turizmu-v-2026-godu-vydelyat-614-mlrd-rubley-68da8468a375c/)
  - The Ministry of Economic Development (Минэкономразвития) runs the 2025-2027 selection — [economy.gov.ru](https://www.economy.gov.ru/material/directions/turizm/minekonomrazvitiya_rossii_prodlevaet_konkursnyy_otbor_investicionnyh_proektov_po_sozdaniyu_modulnyh_nekapitalnyh_sredstv_razmeshcheniya_na_period_2025_2027_godov.html)
  - Regional rounds, e.g. Tatarstan, accept documents until 10 April 2026 — [tourism.tatarstan.ru](https://tourism.tatarstan.ru/subsidii-na-sozdanie-modulnih-nekapitalnih-7710869.htm)
  - Rostov region receives 89.1 M ₽ for modular glamping hotels in 2026-27, with a focus on the M-4 "Don" highway corridor. Glamping was more than 70% of regional accommodation search queries last summer — [RBC Rostov, 15 Aug 2025](https://rostov.rbc.ru/rostov/freenews/689f194a9a79478a08fd9385)
  - An aggregator lists Rosturizm SME grants up to 3.5 M ₽ for modular hotels and concessional loans at 3-5% (secondary source) — [basinn.ru](https://basinn.ru/blog/subsidii-dlya-turisticheskogo-biznesa-2025-2026/)
- Manufacturers market against these programmes. Apple House states its units qualify for state subsidies and grants for tourist bases. DublDom promotes family, IT, rural and basic IZhS mortgages from 6%, loans up to 12 M ₽ in Moscow/SPb and 6 M ₽ elsewhere — [dubldom.ru](https://dubldom.ru/); [apple house](https://xn----8sb5apnlm4d.xn--p1ai/)
- The modular construction market grew 34% in 2023 (citing an RBC rating; secondary) — [T-Bank Secrets](https://secrets.tbank.ru/blogi-kompanij/modulnye-doma-dlya-glempinga/)
- DublDom's 2025 trends piece names expandable modules, smart home, furnished turnkey modules, young families as buyers, and companies investing in campsites — [dubldom.ru trends](https://dubldom.ru/trendy-modulnogo-stroitelstva-v-rossii-v-2025-godu)

### Inferences
- Demand for cabins from glamping operators is underpinned by the 2025-2027 МНСР subsidies. With no 2028 funding planned so far, B2B orders may peak in 2026-27.
- Tighter tax rules and falling occupancy in mature regions (Urals) could push operators toward cheaper and resale units.
- Price inflation since 2022 (about ×1.5 per T-Journal) has pushed small premium units to 3-5 M ₽.

### Gaps
- **Sanctions effects**: I found no reliable source on how sanctions affect this segment (imported glazing, windows, PVC membranes or equipment).
- **Market size**: no 2025-26 market size in ₽ or units for cabins and A-frames specifically. IMRG sells a paid modular-buildings report; its figures were not accessible.
- I did not obtain the Bronevik × Glamping Association 2025 PDF, which is linked from the association page.
