# Russian Bedroom Furniture Sector (спальные гарнитуры, модульные спальни, кровати, шкафы, комоды, тумбы) — 2025-2026

Research date: 2026-10-20. Method: web search (Firecrawl), direct fetch of manufacturers' contact pages (curl/Firecrawl). Several manufacturer sites (angstrem-mebel.ru/contacts, lazurit.com, mebelchr.ru, glazov-mebel.ru, mif.ru, silva.ru, stolline.ru/contacts, bbr.ru) were unreachable from the research environment (timeouts / DNS failures / 403), so some contacts come from search-result snippets or third-party directories and are marked accordingly.

Legend: **[V]** = seen on the manufacturer's own website during this research; **[S]** = from a search snippet of the official site (not opened in full); **[3P]** = third-party directory / dealer / social page — UNVERIFIED, confirm before use.

## Q1. Top manufacturers of bedroom furniture in Russia (+ Belarus): contacts, dealer terms, collections, prices

### Takeaway
The mass corpus-furniture bedroom segment is led by large multi-category holdings (Шатура, Ангстрем, Лазурит, Дятьково, Мебель Черноземья, Black Red White RU, Мария) plus Penza/Volga-region volume producers (Лером, МиФ, SV-Мебель) and bed/mattress specialists (Аскона, Орматек). Most publish B2B/dealer channels (dedicated e-mails), but almost none publish wholesale discount terms openly; MiF is a rare case advertising dealer markups of 50-300%.

## Q3. Segment trends 2024-2026, import substitution, clusters, materials, demand

### Takeaway
After strong growth 2022-2024 driven by IKEA's exit (import share fell from ~41% in 2020 to ~19-21%), the market cooled in 2025 under a 20%+ key rate: wholesale sales −6.6% to 531.4bn RUB, retail units forecast −10%. Bedroom wood furniture was the fastest-growing category during the import-substitution boom; metal beds keep growing.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 wholesale furniture sales −6.6% (−37.5bn) to 531.4bn RUB; ЦФО 188.1bn RUB (35.4%), ПФО 178.2bn — [TAdviser](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/Статья:Производство,_продажа,_экспорт_и_импорт_мебели_в_России)
- Bed production 2025 −0.3% to 3.6m units; wooden adult beds: ЦФО 45.4% (984,304), ПФО 913,507; metal beds +9.1% y/y, +43% vs 2022, ПФО 60.4% of metal beds — same source
- Mattress production 2025 down to 8.0m units (−44% stated) — same source (figure looks extreme; treat with caution)
- ACG: 2025 market in "structural restructuring", output declining after four years of growth — same source
- Production Jan–Apr 2025 −1.6% y/y; full-year forecast −3% in units — [anvikor.ru](https://anvikor.ru/blog/analiz-mebelnogo-rynka-v-rossii)
- Retail furniture sales 2025 may fall ~10% in units vs 2024 — [RIA Недвижимость](https://realty.ria.ru/20251209/mebel-2059718328.html); Forbes cites association forecast of ~509.1bn RUB producer revenue — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.ru/biznes/524652-analitiki-sprognozirovali-snizenie-prodaz-mebeli-v-magazinah-v-rossii-po-itogam-goda)
- AMDPR: 75.7m units produced in 2024 (+12.6% y/y) — [bzbroker.ru](https://bzbroker.ru/potoksss/tpost/jkb3c05et1-rinok-mebeli-rossii-2025-obzor-analitika)
- Production 2020→2024: 227→522bn RUB; imports share 41.3% (2020) → 20.7% (2024) → 18.7% (2025 forecast); ~20 factories involved in IKEA import substitution; former IKEA plant (Новгородская обл.) now makes boards under brand Extravert; ~70% of equipment is European (sanctions risk) — [Деловой профиль](https://delprof.ru/press-center/open-analytics/analiz-strategicheskikh-riskov-mebelnogo-sektora-v-2025-godu/)
- Wood bedroom furniture output grew fastest, +44.1% y/y (during import-substitution phase) — [tpmag.ru](https://tpmag.ru/articles/neprodovolstvennyij-ritejl/trendy-importozamesheniya-v-mebelnyoy-industrii/)
- Former IKEA suppliers: Лузалес-Тихвин +88% output early 2025; Лузалес-Вятка 10× in 2024 — [Inc. Russia](https://incrussia.ru/understand/notikea/)
- Rosstat: 13.59m units of "шкафы кухонные, для спальни, столовой и гостиной" — [TAdviser](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/Статья:Производство,_продажа,_экспорт_и_импорт_мебели_в_России)
- Furniture producer prices rising (+0.6% m/m in April 2025) — [industrymebel.ru](https://industrymebel.ru/analitycs/situacziya-na-rynke-mebeli-2025-kakie-trendy-k-nej-priveli/)

### Inferences
- Clusters: Центральный (Москва обл., Шатура, Ковров/Аскона, Иваново/Орматек, Брянск/Дятьково, Воронеж/Ангстрем & Мебель Черноземья), Поволжье (Пенза, Ижевск/Глазов, Нижний Новгород), Kaliningrad (Лазурит, ИнтерДизайн), Северо-Запад (former IKEA supply base). Materials: LDSP/MDF dominate mass corpus bedrooms; solid wood in premium (Пинскдрев, Дятьково).
- 2026 demand weak due to high rates and mortgage slowdown; consolidation expected (anvikor forecast — speculative).

### Gaps
- No bedroom-specific market size in RUB found for 2025-2026; no published share of marketplaces in bedroom sales.
